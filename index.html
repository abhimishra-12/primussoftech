About Us

A display & software company, built the same way we build our boards — layer by layer.

Primus Softech Solution LLP is an ISO 9001:2015 certified IT company established in 2016. We manufacture, design and develop electronic LED display boards and signage — Digital Display Boards, LED Matrix Displays, LED Message Displays, LED Moving Displays, LED Sign Displays and Train Information Display Systems — for indoor and outdoor use across India.

Alongside hardware, our engineering team builds the software that drives every board: content management, scheduling and remote control, so each sign keeps running long after installation.

  • Dedicated R&D for new display technology
  • In-house strategic engineering team
  • Support that continues after installation, not just before the sale
